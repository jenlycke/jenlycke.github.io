UI Design Gallery


Web interfaces designed for businesses and services. Each case was customized, but the general process I followed included:

  • Meeting with businesses to determine requirements, research current and proposed branding, perform market research, and learn more about the business and the industry
  • Determine KPIs to measure success
  • Competitive analysis of other related businesses
  • Heuristic evaluation to determine what's working and what is not, as far as current user journeys, interface design, and branding
  • Determination of information architecture
  • Site map creation and sign-off
  • Low fidelity wireframes
  • Regular meetings and communication with the business
  • High fidelity mockups, including custom photography and graphic design (in some cases)
  • Design sign-off for development
  • Front-end development in HTML/CSS/JavaScript (in some cases)
  • Final sign-off for launch
  • Analyze and measure success according to the KPIs and/or determine room for improvement
  • Regular design/content updates as necessary (in some cases)
